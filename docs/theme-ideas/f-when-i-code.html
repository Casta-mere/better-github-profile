F · "When I code"

A day-by-day calendar next to an hour-of-day punch card: personality from habits rather than totals. Good when star counts are modest.

Data it needs: commit counts by hour of day and weekday, and daily calendar cells: not in the snapshot. Needs a schema change (schemaVersion: 2) first.

An HTML mockup of the idea, not a theme: a theme draws the same design as an SVG string (see CONTRIBUTING.md, "Add a theme"). The numbers come from the public test fixture, test/fixtures/snapshot-casta-mere.json. The day cells, favorite day and punch card are invented from the weekly totals to show the shape.
